# Get featured in the upcoming Line 47 album artwork

We're upcoming Line 47 album, _As you were meant to go on_ Generate a visual in the browser, save a screenshot, and submit it. Selected images may appear in official artwork and promos—with credit.

No forms, no personal data other that a timestamp and location.
Post your PNG in #submissions on Discord or email.
Include your @handle if you want credit.

[Create and submit your image](/music/aywmtgo/)

## How it works
1. **Create** an image of yourself in the app by adjusting the sliders.

1. **Capture** your image in the app by clicking the save screenshot button. The larger the better.

1. **Submit** your PNG in Discord #submissions. Add a title and your @handle for credit (optional).

1. **Feature**
We’ll showcase submissions leading up to the release and may select pieces for including in the album artwork.
