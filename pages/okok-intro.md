# Get featured in the upcoming Line 47 album artwork

The artwork for the upcoming Line 47 album, _As you were meant to go on_ is going to include submissions from you! 

To participate you'll need to generate an image using the visualizer, save your screenshot, and submit it to dropbox with your name and email. 

Selected images may appear in official artwork and promos.

## How it works
1. **Create and save** an image of yourself [using the visualizer](/music/okok/).

1. **Submit** your image using [the Dropbox form](https://www.dropbox.com/request/vkFZRdu5aacmK3CxCSOB). You'll be asked for your name and email. **_By submitting, you agree to these rules and the license._**

1. **Submissions will be featured** leading up to the release and select submissions will be included in the album artwork.
