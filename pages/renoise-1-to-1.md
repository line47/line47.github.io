## Who this is for

These sessions are for producers who want hands-on help with Renoise and experimental electronic music production.

This is a good fit if you:

* Are new to Renoise and want help getting started
* Come from Ableton, Logic, FL Studio, or hardware and want to understand tracker workflow
* Want to make jungle, breakcore, IDM, acid, drum & bass, or experimental electronic music
* Want to chop breaks, program beats, and work with samples in a more creative way
* Have loops started but struggle to turn them into finished tracks
* Want direct feedback on your Renoise project and workflow

You do not need to be advanced. Sessions can be beginner-friendly, project-focused, or centered around a specific production technique.

## How it works

Each session is 60 minutes and takes place live over Zoom.

You choose the topic you want to focus on, or we can build a custom path based on your goals. You can book one session for focused help or purchase multiple sessions to build a deeper Renoise workflow.

Each session includes:

* 60-minute live Zoom session
* Screen sharing
* Student-selected topic
* Renoise workflow help

If you already have a Renoise project started, you can bring it to the session and we can work through it together.
