I'm currently a Principal Product Designer at Skylight, working on product design, strategy, modernization, and accessibility for the Administration for Children and Families. Before that I led design at Ad Hoc (VA, CMS), was an Innovation Specialist at 18F, and spent ten years as Creative Director at NIC Inc. building Kentucky's state websites.

My style is low-ego and direct. I share work early, make decisions visible, and try to leave teams with practices that keep working after I've moved on.
